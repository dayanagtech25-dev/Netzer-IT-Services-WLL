About Netzer IT Services

Built in Bahrain, for Bahrain. Based in Manama.

Our story

Netzer IT Services was founded by two professionals who saw a gap: too many Bahraini small and medium businesses were settling for unreliable IT infrastructure. We started Netzer to change that.

Jenith and Irfan, one with deep roots in IT support and the other with years inside Bahrain's corporate sector, kept seeing the same problem. Businesses were running on poorly planned infrastructure, suffering unnecessary downtime and paying for reactive fixes instead of planned solutions.

Between them, they had seen what good IT looks like from the inside: the structured, reliable, well-documented infrastructure that large enterprises and financial institutions take for granted. They founded Netzer to bring that same standard to every business in Bahrain, whatever its size.

Our mission

We want every business in Bahrain to know what a properly built IT environment feels like: fast, reliable, secure and ready to grow with you. No shortcuts. No guesswork. Just solid infrastructure, done right.

Our work covers structured cabling, fiber optic installation, IT rack setup, network testing, CCTV security and annual maintenance, for businesses in Manama, Muharraq, Riffa, Seef, Juffair, Isa Town, Hamad Town and across Bahrain.
