About Netzer IT Services

A Bahrain team for cabling, racks, POS and IT support.

Who we are

Netzer IT Services WLL is an IT company in the Kingdom of Bahrain. We build and look after the network that businesses depend on every day: the cables in the walls, the racks in the back room, the POS at the counter and the computers on the desks.

Most network problems start with poor cabling. A loose connector, a cable run too long, or an unlabelled rack can slow a whole office down. We fix that at the source, with clean installation and proper testing.

Our customers include restaurants, retail stores and offices. We know a restaurant cannot wait days for a printer fix, and an office cannot lose its network in the middle of the week. So we answer fast and plan work around your hours.

What we do

Our main work is structured cabling and fiber optic installation. Around that we set up IT racks, support restaurant POS systems, test and certify cabling, and run annual maintenance plans. You can read about each one on our services page.

Where we work

We take jobs in Manama, Muharraq, Riffa, Seef, Juffair, Isa Town, Hamad Town and across Bahrain. If your site is outside these areas, send us a message and we will let you know.
