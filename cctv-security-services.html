CCTV Security Services FAQ

How many CCTV cameras does my shop or office need?

It depends on the size of the place and the number of entrances. We walk the site, mark areas such as doors, counters and parking, and suggest the number of cameras.

Can I watch the cameras on my phone?

Yes. We set up the recorder so you can view live and recorded video from your phone, protected by a password.

Can you add cameras to my existing CCTV system?

In most cases yes. We check your recorder and cables, then add new cameras or replace faulty ones.

How long is the footage stored?

It depends on the hard disk size and the camera settings. We work out the storage you need before we install.
