IT Rack Setup FAQ

What size rack do I need?

A small office usually fits in a 9U to 12U wall cabinet. We suggest leaving 20 to 30 percent free space for growth.

Can you fix a rack that is already a mess?

Yes. We trace each cable, label it and re-route it. We plan the work for a quiet time so you do not lose connection for long.

Do you supply the rack?

We can supply the rack and the parts, or work with equipment you already have.
